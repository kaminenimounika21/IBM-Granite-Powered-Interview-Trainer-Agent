              Technical • Microservices
              Intermediate
            
      
            
      How do you ensure data consistency across distributed microservices without distributed two-phase commit locks?
      
            
      
              IBM Granite Model Answer:
      
              In modern distributed microservices, we avoid synchronous Two-Phase Commit (2PC) locks because they create single points of failure
              and severe latency penalties. Instead, we implement the Saga Pattern. A Saga is a sequence of local transactions where
              each microservice updates its own local database and emits an event or message. If a step fails, the saga executes compensating transactions
              to undo previous changes. We can orchestrate sagas either through choreography (event-driven via Kafka/RabbitMQ) or orchestration (a central coordinator
              managing state machines).
            
      
            
      
              Key Evaluation Criteria: Saga pattern definition • Event-driven choreography vs orchestration • Compensating transactions.
            
      
          
      

          
      
            
      
              HR & Behavioral
              STAR Method
            
      
            
      Tell me about a time you made a mistake on a team project. How did you handle it?
      
            
      
              IBM Granite Model Answer:
      
              Situation: During a sprint deployment, I mistakenly merged a configuration change that pointed our staging service to an unindexed database view.
      
              Task: Our automated integration tests began failing, blocking 3 teammates from validating their features.
      
              Action: I immediately took ownership, reverted the commit within 10 minutes to unblock the team, and conducted a blameless post-mortem. I added a pre-merge CI linting check to ensure all view queries validate index coverage automatically.
      
              Result: We eliminated that failure category entirely, and the team completed the sprint with zero production defects.
            
      
            
      
              Key Coaching Tip: Never blame teammates or hide errors. Senior interviewers prioritize intellectual honesty, rapid remediation, and systemic prevention.
