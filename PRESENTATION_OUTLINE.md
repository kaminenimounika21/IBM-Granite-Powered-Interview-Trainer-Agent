### Slide 2: Problem Statement & Industry Significance
- **The Core Problem:** Technical interview preparation is fragmented, static, and disconnected from candidate resumes.
- **Industry Impact:**
  - 68% of fresh graduates fail initial technical screenings due to poor foundational problem-solving or communication.
  - Resume screening ATS systems reject candidates without transparent feedback on missing skills.
  - Existing generic AI chatbots suffer from hallucinations and lack enterprise grounding.
- **Our Mission:** Democratize enterprise-level interview training for every student and professional using verified IBM technologies.

---

### Slide 3: Target Users & Real-World Pain Points
- **1. Students & Freshers:** Preparing for campus placements; need confidence in DSA, DBMS, OS, Networks, and HR answers.
- **2. Experienced Professionals:** Transitioning roles; need to articulate distributed system trade-offs, microservice failure modes, and leadership scenarios.
- **3. Active Job Seekers:** Need automated resume gap diagnosis against real job descriptions and structured daily roadmaps.

---

### Slide 4: Solution Overview: The Interview Trainer Agent
- **Core Concept:** An intelligent, multi-turn AI coach that combines document intelligence, vector retrieval, and foundation model reasoning.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - IBM Docling resume layout parsing & prerequisite gap detection.
  - RAG knowledge system grounded in IBM technical and behavioral standards.
  - Timed interactive mock interview with real-time browser voice speech recognition.
  - 6-criterion answer evaluation with model answers and beginner-friendly versions.
  - Automated 7-day personalized preparation roadmaps.

---

### Slide 5: System Architecture (The 6 IBM Layers)
- **Layer 1: User Interface:** IBM Carbon Design System SPA (15 complete views, zero external bloat).
- **Layer 2: Document Processing:** IBM Docling Core 2.106.0 document tree extraction.
- **Layer 3: Retrieval & Knowledge:** IBM Langflow RAG graph with IBM Slate dense embeddings (`ibm/slate-30m-english-rtr`).
- **Layer 4: AI Foundation Intelligence:** IBM Granite 3.0 (`ibm/granite-3-8b-instruct`) on watsonx.ai.
- **Layer 5: Agent Orchestration:** IBM watsonx Orchestrate skill specification.
- **Layer 6: IBM Cloud Infrastructure:** IBM Code Engine container runtime, IBM COS, and persistent Cloudant storage.

---

### Slide 6: Resume Intelligence via IBM Docling
- **How It Works:**
  - Accepts PDF, DOCX, and TXT resumes.
  - Ingests into hierarchical `DoclingDocument` representation (headers, lists, tables).
  - Matches candidate credentials against a comprehensive 6-domain technical skill taxonomy.
  - Performs automated gap analysis against the target role benchmark.
  - Outputs an objective Profile Readiness Score (0–100) and structured Markdown document tree.

---

### Slide 7: RAG Workflow via IBM Langflow & IBM Slate
- **The RAG Pipeline:**
  - Document ingestion with Docling.
  - Semantic chunking with overlap.
  - 384-dimensional dense vector embeddings generated by IBM Slate.
  - Cosine similarity vector search over curated technical, HR, and IBM company docs.
  - Dynamic prompt synthesis with verifiable citations grounding IBM Granite generation.

---

### Slide 8: IBM Granite Foundation Model Generation
- **Foundation Model:** `ibm/granite-3-8b-instruct` (Granite 3.0) via watsonx.ai SDK.
- **Prompt Engineering Strategy:**
  - Grounded context injection.
  - Role-specific and difficulty calibration (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
  - 4 Question categories: Technical (DSA/Cloud/SQL), HR, Behavioral (STAR), and Scenario-Based.
  - Multi-part output: Question text, expected criteria, model answer, explanation, follow-up questions, and interview tips.

---

### Slide 9: Interactive Mock Interview & 6-Criterion Scoring Engine
- **Mock Simulation Features:**
  - Question-by-question stepper with countdown timer.
  - Integrated browser speech recognition (voice-to-text) for realistic spoken practice.
- **Objective 6-Criterion Evaluation Breakdown:**
  1. Technical Correctness (1–10)
  2. Relevance (1–10)
  3. Clarity (1–10)
  4. Communication (1–10)
  5. Completeness (1–10)
  6. Problem-Solving Approach (1–10)
- Instant feedback with strengths, improvement areas, and enhanced sample answers.
